Processing

Orders placed before 2pm on a working day are packed the same day. You'll get an email with a tracking link as soon as your parcel leaves our warehouse.

Damaged on arrival

Glass shades and bulbs are double-boxed, but accidents happen. If anything arrives damaged, send us a photo within 7 days and we'll send a replacement free of charge. There's no need to return the broken item.

Changed your mind? You have 60 days to return any item, and returns are free.

How to return an item

  • Start a return from your order confirmation email or account page.
  • Print the prepaid label we send you, or show the QR code at a drop-off point.
  • Pack the item securely, in its original packaging if you still have it.

What can't be returned

  • Bulbs that have been installed and used, unless they're faulty.
  • Made-to-order or custom-length pendants.
  • Items damaged by incorrect installation.

Refunds

Refunds go back to your original payment method within 5–7 working days of the item reaching us. If you used a discount code, the refund reflects the price you actually paid.

Warranty

Every light fitting is covered for 5 years, and LED bulbs for 3 years.

What's covered

  • Faults in materials or manufacturing, including wiring, switches and fittings.
  • Built-in LEDs that fail or lose more than 30% of their brightness.
  • Smart bulbs that stop connecting because of a hardware fault.

What's not covered

  • Normal wear, such as fading of fabric shades or patina on brass.
  • Damage from accidents, misuse, or installation that doesn't follow our instructions.
  • Outdoor use of products that aren't rated for outdoor use.

Making a claim

Email us your order number, a short description of the problem and a photo or video if you can. We'll repair or replace the item, or refund it if neither is possible. Your statutory rights aren't affected.

Hard-wired fittings such as pendants should be installed by a qualified electrician. Keep your installation receipt in case you need to claim.

Privacy

We collect only what we need to run your order and improve the shop. We never sell your data.

What we collect

  • Order details: your name, delivery address, email address and phone number.
  • Payment: handled by our payment provider. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Usage: pages you visit and items you view, collected through cookies if you allow them.
  • Newsletter: your email address, but only if you subscribe.

How we use it

  • To process, deliver and support your orders.
  • To send order updates and, if you opted in, our monthly newsletter.
  • To prevent fraud and keep the site secure.
  • To understand what's popular so we can improve the range.

Who we share it with

We share data only with the services that help us run the shop, such as couriers, our payment provider and our email platform. Each one may use it only for that service.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, download or delete your personal data at any time. You can unsubscribe from emails using the link in any newsletter. To make a request, contact us.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for 7 years for tax purposes. Marketing data is deleted within 30 days of you unsubscribing.

Terms of sale

The basics of buying from Light Shop.

Orders

Your order is a request to buy. A contract is formed when we email you to confirm your order has shipped. If an item turns out to be out of stock, we'll let you know and refund you in full.

Prices

All prices are shown in US dollars and include applicable taxes unless stated otherwise. If we make a pricing mistake, we'll contact you before your order ships, and you can choose to cancel.

Discount codes

Codes can't be combined, exchanged for cash or applied to past orders. Each code has its own expiry date and conditions, which are shown where the code is offered.

Product safety

Always follow the installation and bulb wattage guidance supplied with your product. Light Shop isn't responsible for damage caused by incorrect installation or use.

Changes to these policies

We may update these policies from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when they last changed. The version in place when you ordered applies to that order.
